## What this policy covers

This policy explains how the ZPad app and the website zpad.xphub.dev handle visit-related information. It does not cover third-party sites such as GitHub or the MyGo website, which have their own privacy policies.

## The app

ZPad works on your files locally: nothing is uploaded, analyzed or synced, and there is no telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking. The only network request is the update check against GitHub, which carries just the version number and platform. File names, paths and contents never leave your device.

## Website access logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To keep it reachable and defend against attacks, Cloudflare processes access logs, including IP address, User-Agent, time and request path, under its own privacy policy.

## Visit statistics

The site uses a self-hosted counter to see how the download page and each release are used. It records the page visited, the referrer, an approximate country or region inferred from the IP, device and browser type, and the time. The IP is not written into reports and is not joined with other data.

- No cookies, and no identifiers stored in your browser.
- No cross-site tracking, no advertising profiles, nothing shared with or sold to third parties.
- Statistics are kept only as long as needed for aggregate analysis, never as a long-term archive.
- To turn statistics off entirely: use private mode, block the script, or block the analytics domain.

## Local storage

The site stores one preference in your browser's localStorage — light or dark — to keep the page consistent. It is not used to identify you, and clearing browser data removes it. The app writes nothing to browser storage.

## Third-party links

Download buttons lead to GitHub Releases, the source and feedback links point to GitHub, and the footer links to the MyGo website. Those sites are run by third parties and their own privacy policies apply.

## Sharing and security

We do not collect, sell or share personal information. The site and the statistics service may process access logs outside your country or region; the whole site is served over HTTPS, and collection stays at the minimum needed to run and analyze it.

## Children

ZPad is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect anyone's age information.

## Your rights and contact

The site keeps no data that identifies an individual, so a specific visit usually cannot be looked up or deleted per person. If you have questions, objections or a complaint about how information is handled, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/helloxz/zpad/issues) and we will explain the handling.

## Changes to this policy

This policy changes with the product. The current version is always on this page, with the date at the top, and material changes to statistics or third-party services are noted there.
